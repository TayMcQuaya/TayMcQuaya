~ About Me ~

LORE

I'm Tayfun. I dropped out of college, moved to Bali, and started building. Nothing against theory. I just couldn't ignore what the entire internet was making possible outside the lecture hall.

In 2024 I went all in on vibe coding as a full time pursuit, learning by doing every single day. Six spoken languages. All self taught, all from curiosity.

Pixel art and game dev is where I started. Every character, animation, and game asset on this site was drawn by hand, one pixel at a time (the profile pic being the one exception).

It was meditative, precise, and deeply satisfying. The foundation everything else got built on.

2020 changed everything. Crypto and NFTs pulled me into Web3.

I found communities and builders who showed me what's possible when you follow your curiosity and keep building.

Now I build whatever catches my fire.

Games, SaaS tools, websites, Telegram bots, whatever the idea demands.

If I can dream it, I ship it.

I get better every day. This site is a snapshot, not the full picture.

The best stuff? It's still loading.
