Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

The short version: this site has no analytics, no ads, no cookies and no accounts. I don't collect anything about you. The rest of this page covers the few technical things that happen when a page loads.

1. Who runs this site

This is the personal website of TayMcQuaya. If you have a question about anything on this page, write to taymcquaya@gmail.com.

2. What I collect

Nothing. There are no forms, no sign-ups, no visitor statistics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind on this site.

3. Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub receives your IP address and some technical details (browser, time, the page you asked for) in order to deliver the page and keep its service secure, and it may keep them in its logs. I have no access to those logs. How GitHub handles them is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies.

It keeps one small item in your browser's local storage, called tay-contributions-v1. It holds the public contribution counts of my GitHub account, so the page doesn't have to fetch them again for a few hours. It contains nothing about you and is never sent anywhere. You can remove it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

5. Requests to other services

  • Contribution numbers. To show current numbers, your browser asks a public service that I don't run, github-contributions-api.jogruber.de, for the public contribution count of my GitHub account. As with any request on the web, that service sees your IP address and browser type. Nothing else is sent.
  • Games and demos. When you press Play, the game opens in a frame from the place it is hosted (taymcquaya.github.io or hopline.xyz). Your browser only contacts that host once you press the button. A game may save its own progress in your browser.
  • Everything else. Fonts, scripts, pictures and video clips come from this site itself, not from a font service or a content network.

6. Links to other sites

The site links to other places, such as GitHub, X, LinkedIn, YouTube and the projects I worked on. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies. I'm not responsible for what they do.

7. If you write to me

If you send me an email or a message, I receive your address and whatever you write. I use it to answer you and for nothing else.

8. Changes

If the site starts doing something that isn't described here, I will update this page first. The date at the top shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: taymcquaya@gmail.com.
